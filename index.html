Background

About Me

I'm Rayan Kuribayashi — a Grade 11 student from Matsumoto, Nagano, Japan, currently on a study abroad program at Strathmore High School in Alberta, Canada.

My two core interests are aviation and artificial intelligence. I don't just follow them — I build things with them. The ANA Tokyo Mega Hub started as a notepad of route ideas I carried everywhere. FlightDeck came from wanting a social platform that actually understood what aviation enthusiasts care about. ISN Vocab Bridge came from wanting to do something real for the students at my former school in Japan.

I work as an AI intern at willB Inc. under the supervision of Ryohei Hirabayashi, where I build applications, support business-efficiency projects, and co-teach a biweekly AI literacy course for Grade 6 students at ISN. About 400–450 hours a year across all of it.

Long-term, I'm working toward a career as a commercial pilot through the JAL/ANA cadet program in Japan — with a CS/AI undergraduate degree as the foundation.
